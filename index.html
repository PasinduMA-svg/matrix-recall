How to play
  • Tiles light up on the grid. Remember where they were.
  • When the timer starts, tap every tile that lit up.
  • On order rounds the tiles light one at a time. Tap them in the same order.
  • A wrong tap or running out of time costs a life.
  • Every correct tap and every cleared level scores points. Higher levels are worth more, and finishing quickly adds a time bonus.
  • Clear rounds in a row to raise your score multiplier (up to ×3). Every 5 in a row restores a life.
  • Play once a day to keep your daily streak going.
